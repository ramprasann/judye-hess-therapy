Core Focused Family Therapy: Moving from Chaos to Clarity

By Judye Hess, Ph.D. and Ross Cohen, LPC | Published by Idyll Arbor, Inc. (2008)

Designed as a paradigm shift for students and practicing clinicians who favor an experiential, humanistic approach to working with families. Rather than viewing systemic turmoil as an unmanageable barrier, this text guides therapists in cutting through immediate relational static to uncover the pivotal, unresolved core themes driving household discord.

Featuring verbatim transcripts of unscripted live sessions with actual student families, Dr. Hess and Ross Cohen illuminate how moment-to-moment emotional transparency and structured role clarification can transmute prolonged domestic chaos into systemic clarity, autonomy, and lasting intimacy.
